# Azeroth Signature: your signature in Azeroth

**Make your mark on every message.**

Azeroth Signature automatically adds a signature of your choice to the chat messages you send, so everyone knows who's talking: `<MyMain>` on your alt, `(( OOC ))` for out-of-character chatter, or anything else you like. Set it once, pick your channels, and forget about it.

## What you get

- **Automatic signature:** type as usual, and your prefix is added when you press Enter
- **Channel control:** Say, Yell, Party, Raid, Instance Chat, Guild, Officer, Whisper and custom channels, one checkbox each (with All / None buttons)
- **Excluded characters:** list the characters that should stay unsigned, or click *Add current*
- **Live preview** in the options, so you see exactly how your messages will look
- **Smart rules:** slash commands are never signed, a message is never signed twice, and nothing is touched during combat
- **Native settings panel** under *Options → AddOns → Azeroth Signature*, plus the `/azsig` shortcut
- **Tiny and standalone:** one small addon, no dependencies

## Ideas for your signature

| Signature | Great for |

| `<MyMain>` | Letting people know who's behind your alt |

| `[Tank]` | Announcing your role in groups |
| `~` | A subtle personal mark |

## Quick start

1. Type `/azsig` to open the settings.
2. **Change the default signature** (it starts as `<MyMain>`).
3. Tick your channels, list any excluded characters.
4. Click **Save Settings**. Changes apply once saved.
5. Chat as usual.

## Compatibility

**Retail 12.0** and **WoW Forever** (tested). Other Classic versions are not officially supported.

## FAQ

**Why wasn't my message signed?** It may be a slash command, the channel may not be ticked, the character may be excluded, you may have been in combat, or the addon is switched off.

**Does the signature count towards the 255-character limit?** Yes, it's part of the message.

**Coming from Name2ChatSafe?** Your settings are imported automatically.

**Can I leave the signature empty to disable it?** No. Untick *Enable automatic signature* instead.
